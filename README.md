## Introduction

Welcome to Lexicron! This is a 2d typing game with special character designs and animations.
The game is all about a simple boss fight, typing as much words as you can until you defeat the boss.

## How to play

- Both you and the boss will have a certain amount of HP.
- Each attack, the Player will have a 2% chance of critical damage, while the Boss will get a 3% chance.
- Type the words that is floating around the screen.
- Each word will have a timer before it expires:
  - Easy: 5 Seconds
  - Medium: 4 Seconds
  - Coding: 4.5 Seconds
- To register the word typed, press ENTER. To delete letters, press BACKSPACE.
- Keep typing correct words until you depleat the boss' HP.

## How to change the words

Look for the file folder that should be in:

```
lexicron/
└── src/
    └── main/
        └── words/
            └── textfiles/
                └── words.txt
```

Inside thet ext file, you can add, replace, and delete words that you'd like.
